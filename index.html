A guessing game for fish

🐟 Fishguesser

You get a photograph and a scientific name. Work out where in the world the fish lives, and click that part of the map.

  1. Look at the fish. Its common name starts blanked out — the photo is the clue.
  2. Pick a region on the map, then Lock in guess.
  3. Five rounds, drawn at random from the collection.

Scoring

  • 5,000 for the right region. Get it wrong and you still score, but it falls away the further off you were — a neighbouring region is worth about 3,000, the far side of the planet almost nothing.

Peeking at the name

  • Free Show half hands back part of the name. Costs you nothing, any time you like.
  • 2 per game Reveal name un-blanks the whole thing. You only get two for the whole game.
  • +500 every round you guess with the name still fully blanked — and half counts as peeking, so the free option does cost you this.
  • +500 for each reveal you never spend. Finish with both and that is 1,000 banked.

A perfect game is 28,500: five regions dead right, five clean guesses, both reveals unused.
